Patient Guidance

Important Appointment Guidelines

To ensure all patients receive punctual, unhurried attention from our physicians, please observe the following hospital visit policies.

Check-in 15 Minutes Early

Allows adequate time for preliminary biometric registration, digital temperature, BP and pulse vitals logging.

Cancellation & Rescheduling

Free rescheduling available up to 2 hours before the scheduled time via SMS link or portal.

Prior Medical Records

Please bring past discharge summaries, scan films, and ongoing medicine strips for optimal diagnosis.
