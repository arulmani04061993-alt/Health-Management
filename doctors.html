Patient Guidance

How To Prepare For Your Specialist Visit

Follow these simple steps before arriving at STACKLY to ensure smooth consultations and zero waiting delays.

Step 1
Book Slot Online

Select your preferred specialist and choose an OPD consultation slot or tele-consult video call.

Step 2
Bring Past Reports

Carry your previous blood test reports, MRI/CT scans, past prescriptions, and current medication list.

Step 3
Arrive 15 Mins Early

Complete brief electronic registration at the front desk and vitals check (BP, SpO2, temperature) with nursing staff.

Step 4
Digital Prescription

Receive a digital prescription directly in your patient dashboard along with cashless pharmacy dispensing.
