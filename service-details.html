Advanced Interventional Cardiology

Cutting-edge catheter-based cardiac interventions with zero incisions

Our Interventional Cardiology service provides minimally invasive treatments for coronary artery diseases, structural heart defects, and valve conditions. Using advanced biplane fluoroscopy and intravascular ultrasound (IVUS), our specialists deliver life-saving interventions with faster recovery times and minimal hospital stay.

What's Included in This Service

Coronary Angiography (Radial)

Day-care procedure via the wrist with immediate mobility and same-day discharge.

Drug-Eluting Stent Implantation

Latest bio-resorbable polymer coronary stents preventing re-narrowing of blood vessels.

Rotablation for Calcified Arteries

Diamond-tipped burr drill pulverizing severe calcium blocks safely.

Peripheral Vascular Interventions

Treating diabetic foot ulcers, carotid artery narrowing, and deep vein thrombosis.

Key Patient Benefits

  • Minimally Invasive: Only tiny needle punctures instead of open-heart surgery.
  • Fast Recovery: Most patients resume light routine work within 48 to 72 hours.
  • 24/7 Primary Angioplasty Team: Door-to-balloon emergency response under 45 minutes.
  • Zero Hidden Costs: Completely cashless insurance support across 30+ providers.
