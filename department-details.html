Cardiology & Cardiac Surgery

Comprehensive tertiary cardiac emergency, diagnostic & surgical center

The Department of Cardiology at STACKLY Hospital offers compassionate, comprehensive cardiac healthcare delivered by leading physicians and cardiac surgeons. From preventive lipid clinics to emergency 24/7 primary angioplasty, our cath labs are equipped with artificial intelligence navigation and zero-radiation mapping systems.

Clinical Programs & Facilities

Biplane Cath Lab Suite

High-resolution coronary angiograms, rotablation, and emergency stenting within a 45-minute door-to-balloon time.

Coronary ICU (CCU)

1:1 dedicated nursing ratio, ECMO support, continuous hemodynamic bedside monitoring, and non-invasive ventilators.

Electrophysiology & Pacemaker

3D cardiac rhythm mapping, radiofrequency arrhythmia ablation, and leadless biventricular CRT-D implants.

Cardiac Rehabilitation

Monitored exercise recovery post bypass surgery, nutritional guidance, and risk reduction therapy.
