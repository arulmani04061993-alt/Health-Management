FAQ

Explore our dedicated services and information.

General Hospital Information

General inpatient ward visiting hours are 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM in the morning and 04:30 PM - 07:30 PM in the evening. For Intensive Care Units (ICU & CCU), to prevent infection, visits are restricted to immediate family members between 05:00 PM - 06:00 PM (one visitor per patient at a time).

We are situated at 123 Healthcare Boulevard, Medical District. We provide 24/7 dedicated multi-level basement parking with complimentary valet parking services for emergency patients and mobility-assisted visitors.

Yes, all hospital entrances, elevators, and corridors are 100% ADA compliant with tactile paving, automated elevators, and complimentary wheelchairs assisted by our patient transport staff stationed at Gates 1, 2, and 4.

Yes, STACKLY operates a 24-hour round-the-clock inpatient and outpatient pharmacy in the central lobby, alongside an automated NABL-accredited diagnostic laboratory and 24/7 blood bank.
Appointments & Consultations

Appointments can be booked easily online through our Appointment Portal or by calling +1 (555) 234-8900. You can reschedule or cancel without penalty up to 2 hours prior to your scheduled slot via the link in your confirmation SMS.

Yes, select "Video Tele-Consultation" on the appointment form. You will receive an encrypted video consult link 10 minutes prior to your time. Digital e-prescriptions are sent immediately to your phone upon consultation conclusion.

Patients with pre-booked online appointments are seen within 10–15 minutes of scheduled time. Walk-in patients are triaged based on urgency and typically seen within 30 to 45 minutes depending on specialty doctor availability.

Yes, routine follow-up reviews within 7 days of the initial consultation to review lab investigation results or adjust medication dosages are completely complimentary.
Billing, Pricing & Insurance

We are empaneled with over 30 leading insurers including Star Health, BlueCross BlueShield, Cigna Global, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, HDFC ERGO, Medi Assist, and Niva Bupa. Our dedicated Insurance Desk in Block A handles instant digital pre-authorizations.

We accept all major Visa, MasterCard, and American Express credit/debit cards, UPI payments, net banking, wire transfers, flexible 0% interest EMI options, and physical cash.

Yes! Our Financial Counseling team provides transparent itemized estimates outlining surgeon fees, operating theater charges, anesthesia, room tariffs, and anticipated medication before admission.
Treatments, Surgeries & Reports

Test reports are automatically uploaded to your secure patient portal and sent via a secure encrypted PDF download link directly to your registered email and mobile number within 2 to 6 hours for routine tests.

Yes, our surgical suites feature the Da Vinci Robotic Surgical System and modern 4K laparoscopic instruments for cardiac bypass, total joint replacement, oncology resections, and bariatric procedures ensuring faster recovery.

Our Multi-Disciplinary Tumor Board and Senior Surgical Committee offer formal second opinions where senior consultants collaboratively review your imaging films, biopsy histology, and treatment plans.
Emergency & Patient Care

Call our 24/7 dedicated Emergency Hotline immediately at +1 (555) 911-0000. Our GPS-dispatched Advanced Cardiac Life Support (ACLS) ambulances are on standby with trained paramedics. Emergency admissions do not require initial paperwork.

Yes, each private deluxe room and suite is furnished with a comfortable attendant sleeper couch. One primary attendant is permitted to stay 24 hours with an issued Attendant Pass.
