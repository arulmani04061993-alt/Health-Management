About & Clinical Experience

Dr. Sarah Jenkins is an internationally acclaimed interventional cardiologist with over 16 years of dedicated practice in transcatheter cardiac therapies, structural heart interventions, and preventive cardiovascular wellness. She leads the Advanced Heart Failure & Valve Center at STACKLY Hospital, having performed more than 4,200 successful angioplasty and valve repair procedures.
