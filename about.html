Who We Are

Healing Begins with Understanding, Science & Care

At STACKLY Hospital, our commitment goes far beyond treatment. We believe in building enduring relationships grounded in empathy, clinical precision, and patient-first ethics.

Milestones of Progress

Our Two-Decade Healthcare Journey

From a dedicated regional clinic to a global leader in clinical technology and surgical innovation.

2005 • Foundation
Founding Day

Started as a 50-bed community surgical and cardiac care center with a mission to bring compassionate, affordable healthcare to families.

2012 • Expansion
First Transplant Wing

Launched the specialized organ transplantation center and achieved international Joint Commission International (JCI) gold quality accreditation.

2018 • Innovation
Robotic Surgery Program

Inaugurated the regional center for minimally invasive robotic-assisted surgery, completing over 5,000 successful procedures in its first three years.

2022 • Specialization
Pediatric & Oncology Tower

Commissioned a brand-new 200-bed pavilion dedicated to pediatric intensive medicine, precision chemotherapy suites, and cellular immunotherapies.

2024 • Smart Care
AI-Integrated Digital Health

Adopted real-time AI radiology detection, paperless electronic medical health records (EMR), and telemedicine patient monitoring apps.

2026 • Present Day
Global Medical Campus

Recognized as a leading destination for international medical tourism, delivering multi-organ clinical care with unparalleled patient satisfaction.
